# Privacy Policy for Video Note Clipper

**Last updated: July 26, 2026**

## Data Collection

Video Note Clipper does **not** collect, store, or transmit any user data.

- All notes you type are copied directly to your local clipboard only.
- No data is sent to any server.
- No analytics, tracking, or cookies are used.
- No personal information is collected.

## Permissions

The extension requests host permissions for `youtube.com` and `bilibili.com` solely to:

1. Detect video elements on the page
2. Read the video title and current playback timestamp
3. Pause the video when the shortcut key is pressed

These operations happen entirely within your browser. No data leaves your device.
